Research Interests

I am an MPhil candidate in the School of Biomedical Sciences at The University of Hong Kong, working in the Chul KWON Lab, a high-throughput RNA biology laboratory. My research focuses on developing CRISPR-Cas13 systems, with a primary responsibility for bioinformatics — including computational design of guide RNAs, high-throughput sequencing data analysis, and systematic evaluation of RNA-targeting systems.

My undergraduate training in basic medicine and bioinformatics at Tianjin Medical University gave me a broad, interdisciplinary perspective. I became especially interested in applying computational methods to investigate cancer-related genomic and epigenetic alterations and tumor immune infiltration. Looking ahead, I aim to combine my bioinformatics expertise with CRISPR-Cas13 engineering to develop integrative tools for understanding cancer mechanisms and identifying potential therapeutic targets.

Academic Skills

Bioinformatics: Programming (R, Python, Bash), methylation and mutation analysis, CNV detection, RNA splicing and alternative polyadenylation (APA) event analysis, RNA-seq downstream analysis (tumor purity filtering, differential gene expression, survival curves and prognostic modeling, pathway enrichment, hub gene selection), tumor subtyping, immune cell infiltration, PPI network analysis, and explainable AI (XAI) techniques including SHAP and LIME.

Wet Lab: Cell culture, plasmid construction and extraction, lentiviral packaging and generation of stable cell lines, transfection, RNA extraction with qPCR and RT-PCR, protein extraction and Western blotting, DNA electrophoresis, immunofluorescence and confocal microscopy, CCK-8 proliferation assay, migration and invasion assays, mouse husbandry and tumor formation models, IHC and histological analysis.

Research Tools: Operating system proficiency (Windows, Linux), version control and containerization (GitHub, Docker), literature search and reference management, data processing and academic writing, presentation and visualization (LaTeX, PowerPoint, Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator).
