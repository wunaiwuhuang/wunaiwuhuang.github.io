- **Explored the impact of DNA methylation on intronic pre-mRNA polyadenylation (IPA) across 23 cancer types.** Investigated how IPA, similar to alternative splicing, generates RNA isoforms with missing exons, potentially producing altered proteins relevant to cancer biology.
- **Constructed a pan-cancer ipaQTM map using TCGA methylation and transcriptome data.** Performed cis- and trans-association analyses with Inpact and MatrixEQTL to link methylation sites to IPA events, identified over 500,000 significant associations, and pinpointed thousands of candidate IPA regulators including genes such as *CCAR2*, *CPSF1*, and *ZC3H3*, as well as regulatory effects involving DNA structure and transcription factor binding (e.g., *CTCF*).
- **Applied integrative computational approaches,** including methylation-QTL mapping, transcriptomic analysis, and regulatory network inference, to systematically link DNA modifications with RNA processing events in cancer.
