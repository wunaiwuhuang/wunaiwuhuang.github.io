Investigated the relationship between DNA methylation and intronic pre-mRNA polyadenylation (IPA) across 23 cancer types using TCGA data, and further explored underlying mechanisms including trans effects from genes such as CCAR2, CPSF1, and ZC3H3, as well as cis effects involving DNA structure and transcription factor binding (e.g., CTCF). Constructed a pan-cancer ipaQTM map identifying over 500,000 cis-/trans- associations and discovered thousands of putative IPA regulators.
