- **Investigated the tumor-suppressive role of *HADH* isoform 3 in endometrial cancer (EC).** Found that isoform 3 is selectively downregulated in EC, and its loss activates EGFR-MEK/ERK signaling to promote tumor progression, suggesting biomarker and therapeutic potential.
- **Designed an independent project integrating computational and experimental approaches.** Analyzed TCGA and GEO datasets to profile fatty acid metabolism genes, applied Lasso and survival-SVM models to identify *HADH* as a prognostic marker, and quantified isoform-specific expression with long-read RNA-seq. Established stable overexpression and knockdown EC cell lines, then assessed proliferation, migration, and invasion using CCK-8, wound healing, and transwell assays. Validated the cholesterol-EGFR-MEK/ERK axis through qPCR, western blotting, and non-denaturing PAGE.
- **Gained proficiency in diverse research methods,** spanning bioinformatics analyses, molecular biology assays, and functional validation, while developing strong skills in experimental planning, data interpretation, and scientific reasoning.
