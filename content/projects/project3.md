Identified the link between the fatty acid metabolism gene HADH and endometrial cancer (EC) using RNA-seq, genomics, and metabolomics data analysis. Discovered HADH isoform 3 is downregulated in EC through DDX3X suppression, leading to activation of the MEK-ERK signaling pathway and promotion of malignant EC phenotypes. Demonstrated the therapeutic potential of HADH as a biomarker for EC, and its role in tumor infiltration and microenvironment construction.
