- **Contributed to the analysis and writing of a high-resolution comparative study of RNA-targeting systems** in the Chul KWON Lab (School of Biomedical Sciences, HKU), and played a substantial role across the whole project.

- **The study developed shRNAone,** an engineered shRNA scaffold that eliminates processing heterogeneity and guarantees guide biogenesis at single-nucleotide precision. It systematically decoupled microRNA seed and 3′ supplementary pairing rules using a library of 2,800 miRNA variants (SynthoMir), demonstrated that potent guides identified by single-nucleotide tiling screens directly translate into synthetic siRNA designs, and mapped the knockdown landscapes of shRNAone, CasRx, and PspCas13b across the identical mRNA — revealing that target RNA accessibility is a universal determinant of guide efficacy.

- **My work focused on computational analyses and manuscript writing,** including high-throughput sequencing and pooled screening data analysis, parametrization of the RNA accessibility model (RNAplfold), and translation of the analyses into the manuscript.
