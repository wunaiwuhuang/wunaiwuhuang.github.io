Investigated the role of Raptor, a core component of the mTORC1 complex, in post-ischemic angiogenesis using both in vivo and in vitro models. Explored the contribution of mTORC1 signaling in vascular regeneration, providing novel insights into the molecular regulation of ischemia-induced angiogenesis.
