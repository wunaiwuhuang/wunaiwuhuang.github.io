About Me

I am an MPhil candidate in the School of Biomedical Sciences at The University of Hong Kong, working in the Chul KWON Lab, where I focus on developing CRISPR-Cas13 systems with a primary emphasis on bioinformatics. I received my Bachelor of Medicine in Basic Medicine from Tianjin Medical University, where my research spanned computational biology and translational cancer research.
